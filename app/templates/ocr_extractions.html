How Enhanced OCR Integration Works with AI Tutor
  • Multi-Format Support: Upload images (JPG, PNG, etc.) or PDF files for text extraction
  • Smart PDF Processing: Direct text extraction for text-based PDFs, OCR fallback for scanned documents
  • Automatic Context: When you chat with the AI tutor, it automatically searches your OCR-extracted text for relevant information
  • Targeted Search: The AI looks for content related to your questions in all extracted text
  • Recent Context: If no specific matches are found, recent extractions are used as general context
  • Enhanced Answers: Responses are enriched with information from your extracted text alongside other knowledge sources
